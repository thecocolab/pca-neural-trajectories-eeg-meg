A Primer on Low-Dimensional Neural Dynamics

PCA-based trajectory analysis for EEG and MEG — interactive analysis reports

Averaging across sensors hides the fact that an evoked response is a path: the sensor pattern moves through a state space over time. These analyses fit a principal-component basis to sensor-space data, plot the resulting condition trajectories, and test how far that compact coordinate system carries — through geometric descriptors, nonlinear-embedding comparisons, frequency-band envelopes, and leakage-safe cross-participant decoding.

Every link below opens a self-contained interactive report: figures, tables, statistics, and the provenance of the run that produced them. Pick a dataset, then a family of analyses.
